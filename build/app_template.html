Reading it

Plan view puts the rear (patio) at the top and the front porch at the bottom, like the drawings; the garage is on the left. Click anything to read what it is: windows and doors carry their A-1 tag, devices their E-1 tag, fixtures and pipes their P-1 tag, radiant loops their R-1 tag. The exterior walls show the siding outside and drywall inside, so turning them off leaves the finished interior. The radiant loops in the slab and the plumbing under it sit below the plan drawing: turn the drawing off to see them.

To point a comment at one thing, pick it and press Comment, or name the sheet and the tag in the chat (A-1 W12, E-1 R12, P-1 WC1, M-1 S3).
