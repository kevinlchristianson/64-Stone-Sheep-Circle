M-1 HVAC · 64 Stone Sheep Circle

Ductless: two cold-climate multi-zone mini-split heat pumps with wall heads; the radiant slab (R-1) carries the heat. App version v14. From the owner's plans (Larson House, 6/11/26).

Notes

  1. Design conditions: Powell, WY (ASHRAE 2009, Cody Muni AWOS (nearest station to Powell), 99.6 % heating / 1 % cooling). Heating -11.4°F, cooling 87.8°F; inside 70°F / 75°F.
  2. No ducts. The radiant slab (R-1) heats every room; the mini-splits cool the rooms with heads and give fast, second-source heat there. Rooms without a head (office, laundry, mud room, powder, halls) take air from the next room through their doors.
  3. Sizing: each head carries the cooling load of the rooms it serves plus 15 % for latent and margin, with 6k the smallest head; the living head is held at 9k for the vault, the open kitchen and cooking. Each outdoor unit is the smallest common cold-climate multi-zone with enough ports, with the heads at no more than 130 % of its rating.
  4. Outdoor units sit on a concrete pad against an exterior wall, raised 12" on a stand for snow, backs 30" off the wall so they are outside the eave drip line and can be serviced; snow guards on the roof above each. CU-1 is in the rear alcove between the master bedroom and the patio; CU-2 is on the Bath 2 wall of the bedroom wing. Line sets were routed for the shortest run from there.
  5. Line sets to heads on exterior walls stay outside: from the unit in a painted line-hide along the wall at about 8' (just above the window heads) and straight through the wall into the back of the head (IU-5, IU-6). The others go through a sealed sleeve about 18" up, rise inside a full-height stud bay to the attic, run on the ceiling framing under the insulation and drop inside the wall behind the head. The living set rides the low edge of the kitchen/living vault at Y 17'-6", where the scissor trusses' bottom chord is only about 6" above the plate, then the Bedroom 1 and hall attics; nothing crosses the middle of the vault.
  6. Heads mount high on the wall, top 6" below the ceiling, on a wall that lets them throw across the room. Exterior-wall heads drain condensate by gravity straight out the wall to a splash block (with the line set where there is one); interior-wall heads (IU-2, IU-3) get a mini condensate pump behind the head that discharges outside with the line set.
  7. Pressure test, evacuate and pull a micron vacuum per the maker. Every sleeve is sealed and flashed; line-hide painted to the siding.
  8. Each outdoor unit has a dedicated 240 V circuit and a fused disconnect on the wall within sight (DS1 on the master bedroom's alcove wall, DS2 beside CU-2 on the Bath 2 wall), sized to the nameplate MCA/MOCP once the units are picked (E-1). The heads are powered from their outdoor unit through the 14/4 interconnect run with the line set.
  9. Wired wall controllers T1-T6 at 48" by each door; set the heads to cool and to heat only below the slab's setpoint so they don't fight the radiant.
  10. Ventilation: with no ducts, the draft assumes continuous low-speed bath fans (exhaust-only, E-1) for the IRC whole-house rate; paired through-wall ERVs are the upgrade (see open items).

Open items

  • CU-1 in the rear alcove: it is 2 ft from the edge of the covered patio and under the dining windows, so it is heard on the patio and in the dining room when it runs. The alternatives are the master bedroom's left wall (quiet, but the living set runs about 105 ft, past the per-head limit) or the front porch (every set under 75 ft, in sight of the street).
  • CU-2 on the Bath 2 wall: 3 ft from Bedroom 2's window W14. The Bath 1 wall is the quiet spot (8 ft from any bedroom window) for about 10 ft more line.
  • Exact models: pick the brand, then confirm the heads are an allowed combination and the line lengths are inside the unit's limits (CU-1: longest 69 ft, total 145 ft; CU-2: longest 57 ft, total 102 ft).
  • Truss maker: the living set crosses the scissor trusses at Y 17'-6"; leave web openings for a 2" bundle there, or run it along a truss.
  • Office: no head as drawn (the ask was the right-side bedrooms). A 6k head on the office's right wall would make CU-2 a 4-port.
  • Bed walls: the master and Bedroom 1 heads sit on the rear wall between the windows, where a headboard may go. A head over a headboard works with its vanes set level; otherwise it moves to a side wall. Confirm furniture walls before rough-in.
  • Ventilation: continuous bath fans (drawn) or paired through-wall ERVs (about 133 CFM continuous under the IRC).
