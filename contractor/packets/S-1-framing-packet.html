S-1 Framing · 64 Stone Sheep Circle

Wall types, rough openings, headers, blocking and the roof truss layout. Walls numbered from 1, exterior first. App version v8. From the owner's plans (Larson House, 6/11/26).

Notes

  1. Datum 0,0 (blue target, top left of the drawing): where the outside face of the garage-door wall (X = 0) meets the line of the outside face of the master bedroom's rear wall (Y = 0). It is the rear-left corner of the layout string lines, not a wall corner: set it from those two lines. The grid is 5'-0" each way from it; X runs to the right (toward the bedroom wing), Y toward the front (the porch). Every sheet uses it.
  2. Walls: 2x6 exterior, 2x4 interior (owner, 2026-10-13), drawn at stud depth (5-1/2" and 3-1/2"). Plumbing walls are 2x4 like the rest; where a 3" drain or vent goes through a plate, strap it (IRC R602.6.1).
  3. Laundry sound walls (green, owner 2026-10-13): 2x6 plates with 2x4 studs alternating faces at 12" o.c. (24" o.c. on each face), R-15 batt woven between, no stud touching both faces. They grow 2" into the laundry, so the master bedroom, master closet, mud room, master hall and powder room keep their A-1 sizes; the laundry is 2" smaller each way. D11 needs a 2x6 pocket frame.
  4. Plate height 9'-1 1/8" (PT plate, 104-5/8" precut studs, double top plate) for 9'-0" ceilings. The kitchen and living vault is in the trusses (4:12 scissor bottom chords); no wall runs past the plate.
  5. Headers: with 8' heads there is 7-5/8" between the rough opening and the top plates, so exterior headers are 7-1/4" deep: 2-2x8 up to a 4' opening, LVL above that (the two 9' garage doors and the 8' patio French doors: engineer to confirm, or raise the plate). Interior walls carry no roof: a flat 2x4 header with cripples (IRC R602.7.4).
  6. Roof: trusses at 24" o.c. bearing on the exterior walls (roof framing schedule). The main trusses span front to rear on the A-2 ridge line; a 2-ply girder truss (G1) on the master-wing/kitchen line carries the master wing and garage trusses, so the wall under it is a partition. Hang every partition top plate with slip clips (no nails into the bottom chords) so truss uplift doesn't crack the ceilings.
  7. Door rough openings: leaf + 2" wide, + 2-1/2" tall; pockets 2 x leaf + 1". Window rough openings: unit + 1/2" each way (confirm with the window maker).
  8. Garage separation: 5/8" Type X on the garage side of the walls to the house and on the garage ceiling; D6 is a 20-minute self-closing door.
  9. Blocking (magenta): the bands on the drawing and the blocking schedule; grab-bar blocking at every toilet, tub and shower; vanity tops and mirrors, the washer box, the TV over the fireplace.

Open items

  • Datum: confirm the 0,0 layout corner with the surveyor's pins before the forms go in.
  • Window sizes and heights are scaled from the elevations; confirm the window schedule with the supplier before framing.
  • Bedroom 3's only window (W17, 4'x4') scales with a 4'-0" sill: above the 44" egress limit. Lower it or use a casement sized for egress.
  • Interior door heights are not on the plans (8'-0" assumed to match the exterior doors on the elevations).
  • Roof trusses: the layout here is a draft from A-2. The truss maker designs the trusses, the hip and valley sets, the G1 girder and its end posts, and the over-framed 8:12 gables at the entry and office.
  • Heel: 12" energy heel assumed (full R-49 to the wall line); it sets the eave height on the elevations.
  • Fireplace insert: chimney chase framing through the vaulted living ceiling and roof; clearances per the insert's manual.
  • Living room label on A-1 says 17'-4" wide; the walls measure about 20'-5". Framing follows the walls.
  • Foundation (slab assumed: the garage and mechanical floor drains, the radiant slab), thickened footings under G1's end posts and the patio and porch posts, anchor schedule.
