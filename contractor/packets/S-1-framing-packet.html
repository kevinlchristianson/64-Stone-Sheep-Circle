S-1 Framing · 64 Stone Sheep Circle

Wall types, door and window rough openings, headers and blocking. Walls numbered W1 up, exterior first. App version v2. From the owner's plans (Larson House, 6/11/26).

Notes

  1. Walls are 2x6 exterior and 2x4 interior as drawn on A-1 (5-1/2" and 3-1/2"); wet walls behind toilets, tubs, showers, sinks and the washer go 2x6.
  2. Plate height 9'-0" (A-2: all interior ceilings 9' except the 4:12 vault over kitchen and living). Precut studs for a 9' wall: 104-5/8".
  3. Roof framing is not in the plan set. Trusses bearing on the exterior walls are assumed; with that, interior walls are non-bearing. The vault over kitchen and living needs scissor trusses or a ridge beam: truss designer or engineer to confirm.
  4. Door rough openings: leaf + 2" wide, + 2-1/2" tall. Window rough openings: unit + 1/2" each way (confirm with the window maker).
  5. Headers on exterior walls sized for a single-story truss roof; the two 9' garage door headers are LVLs to be sized by the engineer or truss/LVL supplier.
  6. Garage separation: 5/8" Type X on the garage side of the walls to the house and on the garage ceiling; D6 is a 20-minute self-closing door.
  7. Blocking: grab bars at every toilet, tub and shower (ADA toilets drawn), towel bars, vanity tops, wall cabinets, TV at the fireplace wall, the curved washer/dryer.

Open items

  • Window sizes and heights are scaled from the elevations; confirm the window schedule with the supplier before framing.
  • Bedroom 3's only window (W17, 4'x4') scales with a 4'-0" sill: above the 44" egress limit. Lower it or use a casement sized for egress.
  • Interior door heights are not on the plans (8'-0" assumed to match the exterior doors on the elevations).
  • Roof: truss layout, vault framing over kitchen/living and the patio's 6:12 vaulted ceiling.
  • Fireplace insert: chimney chase framing through the vaulted living ceiling and roof; clearances per the insert's manual.
  • Living room label on A-1 says 17'-4" wide; the walls measure about 20'-5". Framing follows the walls.
  • Foundation type (slab assumed: the garage and mechanical floor drains) and anchor schedule.
